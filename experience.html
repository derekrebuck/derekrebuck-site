Experience

I’ve rarely had the luxury of staying in one lane. That has been useful.

My career has moved through graphic design, web, digital communications, government, marketing, analytics and front-end work. I am often the only designer in the room, which means I have learned to understand the whole problem instead of waiting for someone else to define my piece of it.

Current role

Web Developer & Graphic Specialist

U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit

I lead much of the court’s visual branding and creative direction while also working across web design, UI and UX, front-end development, communications, photography, video, data and digital accessibility. I have a high degree of autonomy, and people trust me to recommend the direction that will work best rather than simply execute whatever was first requested.

That can mean designing a website, building a campaign, creating signage, coordinating with vendors, helping teams think through visitor flow, or turning a loose idea into something concrete enough for multiple offices to act on.

The common thread

I’m not trying to be everything. I’m good at seeing how the pieces fit together.

Graphic design is still my foundation. The broader skill set makes me better at it because I understand what happens before the design, what happens after it, and what can break in between.
