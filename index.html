How I approach the work

I care about the reason behind the decision.

Good work starts before I open a design program. I want to understand the audience, the message and what the final piece actually needs to accomplish.

Find the logic.I’m good at taking scattered ideas and organizing them into something people can understand and use.
Build accessibility in.Contrast, readability, responsive behavior and structure are part of the design from the beginning.
Keep it intentional.Color, type, hierarchy and interaction should support the work, not compete with it.

Background

Graphic design is the foundation. My work has grown well beyond it.

Over the past decade, I’ve worked across print, digital, web, motion, communications and data for government, health, real estate, education, startups and independent clients.

That mix is useful. It means I can look at a project as more than a single deliverable and think about how the pieces work together.
