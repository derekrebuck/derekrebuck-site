What I do

Graphic design is the foundation. The rest grew from there.

I’ve spent more than a decade designing across government, higher education, real estate, startups and freelance work. Along the way, my practice expanded into web development, accessible digital design, motion, analytics and content strategy.

I like work that has a real problem behind it. Give me a complicated message, a messy system or an audience that needs something to make sense, and that is usually where I do my best work.
