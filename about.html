About Derek

Creative instincts.
Analytical wiring.

I’m a multidisciplinary visual designer and web developer who likes making complicated things feel clear, useful and worth looking at.

Profile

Design is where creativity, strategy and human behavior meet.

I have more than a decade of experience across graphic design, web design and digital communications. My work has supported federal agencies and courts, universities, nonprofits, real estate firms, startups, churches and independent clients.

My education in both graphic design and political science gave me an unusual combination: visual instincts paired with research, writing and systems thinking. Accessibility is part of that same mindset. A design has not solved the problem if people cannot use it.
