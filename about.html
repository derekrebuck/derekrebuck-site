About me

I’m Derek. I make visual ideas work harder.

I’m a multidisciplinary visual designer and web developer based in the DMV. Graphic design is my foundation, but I’ve never been especially interested in staying inside one lane.

How I got here

Creativity on one side. Research and strategy on the other.

I’ve spent more than a decade working across graphic design, web design and digital communications. My work has supported federal agencies and courts, colleges and universities, nonprofits, real estate firms, startups, churches and independent clients.

I also studied political science alongside graphic design. That combination still shows up in how I work. I care about the visual idea, but I also care about the audience, the evidence, the message and whether the final product actually does what it is supposed to do.

Accessibility fits naturally into that. To me, accessible design is not a separate style or a final compliance pass. It is part of making something useful in the first place.

Experience

A career that keeps getting broader.

I’ve moved between print, digital, government, marketing and web work, which is a big part of why I tend to see projects as systems instead of isolated deliverables.

What matters to me

Clear. Useful. Memorable. Accessible.

I like clean systems, strong hierarchy, smart use of color and details that feel intentional. I’m happiest when the final answer looks simple, even if getting there took a lot of thinking.
