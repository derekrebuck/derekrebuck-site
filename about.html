About me

I’m quiet. The work usually isn’t.

I’m Derek, a multidisciplinary graphic designer and web developer based in the DMV. I’m happiest behind the scenes, taking an idea that is still a little messy and figuring out how to make it clear, useful and visually strong.

What I do

Graphic design is my foundation, not my limit.

I’ve spent more than a decade working across graphic design, web design, UI/UX, digital communications, video and data. Most of my career has involved being the person who can move between those areas when a project needs it.

That breadth is less about collecting job titles and more about solving the problem in front of me. Sometimes the answer is a visual identity. Sometimes it is a website, an infographic, a campaign, a better content structure or simply making something easier to use.

I also studied political science alongside graphic design, which probably explains why I enjoy the research and logic behind a project almost as much as the visual part.

How I think

Start with the audience. Then make the design earn its place.

I try to understand what an organization is saying, who needs to hear it, what they need from it and how they should feel when they get there. If those pieces are disconnected, that is usually the first problem to solve.

Accessibility is part of that thinking. I do not want contrast, mobile behavior, readable type or screen-reader structure to become a cleanup task at the end. They belong in the foundation.

Experience

A broad career, connected by design.

I’ve worked in federal service, health communications, the courts, real estate, manufacturing, education, startups and independent projects.

The short version

I like work that is thoughtful, useful and worth being proud of.

I care about the details, but I am practical about them. If something helps the audience or strengthens the idea, it belongs. If it is only there because it looks clever, it probably does not.
