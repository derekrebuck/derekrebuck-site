Intent and purpose are at the core of how I design.

I’m Derek Rebuck, a multidisciplinary designer based in the DMV. Graphic design is my foundation, but my work also spans web, UI/UX, communications, video and data.

Across disciplines

A broader toolkit changes how I see the problem.

I’ve spent more than a decade working across visual design, websites, digital communications, video and data. Most of my career has required moving between disciplines instead of staying in a single lane.

That range helps me focus on what the project actually needs rather than forcing every problem into the same format. Sometimes the right answer is a website. Sometimes it is a visual system, an infographic, a campaign, a video or a combination of several things working together.

I also studied political science alongside graphic design. That background strengthened the research, writing and analytical side of my work and made me comfortable translating dense information into something people can actually use.

How I design
01

Understand the context

I start by getting inside the perspective of both the organization and the audience. Who needs to see this? What needs to be communicated? What should someone understand, feel or do when they are finished?

02

Organize the problem

I take scattered ideas and turn them into a clear direction. I identify what matters most, what can be simplified, and what structure will make the information easier to understand before I begin polishing the visuals.

03

Design for real use

Accessibility, responsive behavior, readable type, contrast and practical constraints are built into the design from the beginning. The final work needs to function as well as it looks.
